Over a Century of Service

Welcome to Eastern Visayas Medical Center

Since 1916, EVMC has been the cornerstone of healthcare in Eastern Visayas , evolving from a modest 14-bed provincial hospital to a premier 1,500-bed capacity medical center serving the entire region.

As a tertiary level teaching and training government hospital , we are committed to delivering globally-competitive, evidence-based healthcare services. Despite the devastation of Super Typhoon Yolanda in 2013, EVMC remained one of the few hospitals in operation , demonstrating our unwavering dedication to the community.
